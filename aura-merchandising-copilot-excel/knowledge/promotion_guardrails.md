# Demo promotion guardrails

These are general demo guidelines, not actual rules of any named retailer.

- A promotion must comply with the selected brand's configured discount cap.
- A promotion should meet the minimum margin requested by the business, unless an authorized user explicitly changes that objective and the policy permits it.
- Never recommend selling more units than available inventory.
- Clearance time is an estimate derived from forecast daily sales; it is not a guarantee.
- Quantity offers must account for free units in inventory depletion and product cost.
- Bundles require available inventory for all bundle components.
- Vouchers and gifts must include estimated redemption and gift costs where known.
- If no strategy meets the deadline and margin constraint, state the shortfall and show the best feasible alternatives.
- Do not invent sales history, stock, cost, product details, policy approvals or campaign results.
