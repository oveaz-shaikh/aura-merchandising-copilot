# Retail decision principles

- Compare scenarios over the same time horizon and stock basis.
- Distinguish baseline sales from incremental sales; a forecast difference is not automatically causal uplift.
- Revenue is not profit.
- Gross profit excludes campaign, reward, voucher, gift, labor and operating costs.
- Report assumptions and uncertainty alongside forecasts.
- Use approved brand guidelines and consented customer data for campaign targeting.
- Review actual campaign results before using a promotion-response model in production.
